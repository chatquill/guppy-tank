## Scanned rocks, wood, plants and ornaments, and gravel textures

From [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), all CC0 (public domain). Models are rendered to sprites by `art-src/decor_scan.py`;
gravel and sand textures are built into the tank floor by `art-src/gen_env.py`.

## Tools

Background removal: [rembg](https://github.com/danielgatis/rembg) (MIT). Rendering: Blender.
